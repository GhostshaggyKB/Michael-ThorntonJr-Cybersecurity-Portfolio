# Work-Based Security Case Study Template

`Work-Based Case Study — Sanitized Incident Reconstruction`

## Confidentiality Statement

This case study is a sanitized reconstruction of a real security-related incident I supported in a professional environment.

All identifying, proprietary, customer, employer, and potentially NDA-covered information has been removed or generalized.

Do not include:

- Employer or customer names
- Employee names
- Real email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Account numbers
- Ticket numbers
- Internal URLs
- Internal hostnames
- Employer IP addresses
- Credentials
- Passwords
- API keys
- Access tokens
- Session cookies
- Proprietary procedures
- Internal network diagrams
- Employer-system screenshots
- Confidential logs

Use generalized terms such as:

- `the organization`
- `User A`
- `Service Desk`
- `Information Security Team`
- `remote-access software`

---

## Executive Summary

Briefly explain:

- What was reported
- Why it appeared security-related
- What you personally did
- Whether you contained, resolved, or escalated it
- What the final outcome was

## Investigation Process

Describe your investigation in chronological order.

For each major step, explain:

1. What you checked
2. Why you checked it
3. What you observed
4. How that affected your next decision

Use first-person language such as:

- `I reviewed...`
- `I investigated...`
- `I identified...`
- `I documented...`

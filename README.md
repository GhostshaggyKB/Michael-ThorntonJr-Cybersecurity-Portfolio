# Professional Approach

My approach to security-related technical work follows a repeatable process:

1. Understand the reported issue and establish the available context.
2. Identify unusual behavior or suspicious indicators.
3. Review evidence available within my authorized access.
4. Correlate observations before drawing conclusions.
5. Determine whether immediate containment, remediation, or escalation is appropriate.
6. Take authorized actions within my professional responsibilities.
7. Document observations, actions, uncertainties, and decisions.
8. Coordinate with specialized security teams when deeper investigation is required.

I focus on evidence-based investigation, clear documentation, appropriate escalation, and accurately distinguishing my direct work from activities performed by other teams.

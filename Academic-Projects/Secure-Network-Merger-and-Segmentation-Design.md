## My Role

I analyzed security and infrastructure considerations and developed a proposed network-security architecture designed to reduce risk while maintaining required business connectivity.

My work included:

- Evaluating network-security risks
- Comparing existing security controls
- Identifying opportunities to reduce exposure
- Designing logical security boundaries
- Applying segmentation principles
- Incorporating stronger identity and authentication controls
- Evaluating secure remote and inter-network connectivity
- Considering cloud services where appropriate
- Applying least-privilege and Zero Trust principles
- Considering compliance and operational requirements
- Evaluating security improvements against implementation constraints
- Creating and documenting a proposed network topology

---

## Security Design Approach

My design emphasized layered security rather than relying on a single defensive control.

I considered how segmentation, authentication, access control, firewalls, encrypted connectivity, cloud security, and reduced network exposure could work together to strengthen the overall architecture.

I also considered the potential impact of credential compromise, malware, lateral movement, vulnerable systems, and inappropriate access when making design decisions.

## What I Learned

This project reinforced that secure network design requires more than connecting systems together.

Security decisions must account for trust boundaries, user access, system exposure, lateral movement, authentication, business requirements, operational continuity, and future growth.

It also strengthened my understanding of how multiple security controls can work together to reduce risk while still supporting business operations.
